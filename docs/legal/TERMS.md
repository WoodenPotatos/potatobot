## 4. The service

### 4.1 What it is

The bot offers, among other things, a virtual economy with coins and
experience, casino-style games, a gacha, a shop, daily puzzles, counting and
word-chain channels, group search, temporary voice rooms, tickets, moderation
tools, announcements, role rules, an event log, stream and patch-note
notifications, and
a dashboard where administrators configure all of this. Every feature can be
switched on or off per server by that server's administrators, so what you see

- You confirm you are entitled to add the bot and grant it the permissions you

  templates say. **You are responsible for those decisions**, for their
  lawfulness, and for telling your members about the features you enable. Link

- Channels that receive member-identifying output (ticket transcripts, the
  invite log, the moderation log, `/modlogs`, the inactivity report) must be
  restricted to staff.
- Automatic moderation actions (timeouts, kicks, bans from thresholds; message
  deletions from the filter) are taken by the software on your configuration.
  You must give members a way to contest them, and you must be able to reverse
  them.
- You use the dashboard only for servers you manage, keep your Discord account
  secure (we recommend two-factor authentication), sign out on shared
  computers, and do not share your session.
- You will not use the dashboard or the staff commands to collect members'
  data for purposes unrelated to running the server.
- If you remove the bot from your server, the server's configuration and your
  members' data stay in the installation under the Privacy Policy's retention
  rules until erased; ask us if you want them removed sooner.
